- **Different:** the local wording selects another behavior, contract, or owner.
- **Locally more specific:** the local wording adds a constraint, mechanism, or
  workflow detail to a broader canonical rule.
- **Locally less specific:** the local wording leaves a condition or obligation
  unstated or less precisely defined.
- **Additional local rules:** independently actionable requirements with no
  equivalent obligation in the compared canonical sections. A rule may also
  specialize a broader principle in section 2; its addition is identified here.

An explicit exception appears under **Different**, even when it also reduces
coverage. More detail does not automatically make a rule preferable. This
comparison concerns documentation; it does not certify implementation behavior.
